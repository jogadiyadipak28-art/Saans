title: Mosquito coils are smoke
source: WHO household air pollution notes, summarized
url: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/household-air-pollution-and-health
tags: myth coil indoor
---

Coils and electric vaporisers are not the same. A burning coil is indoor smoke. On a smog night, prefer a net or a non-burning method you already own. Do not burn a coil in a sealed room.
