---
id: wet-mop
title: Wet mop instead of dry dusting
source: US EPA indoor air guidance, summarized
url: https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq
tags: indoor dust budget
---

Dry dusting lifts settled particles back into the air. A damp cloth or mop holds them. Do floors, window sills, and fan blades. This is a free habit and it still matters in a home that cannot buy a filter.
