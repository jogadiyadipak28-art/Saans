title: Use the chimney you have
source: WHO household air pollution notes, summarized

tags: cooking chimney
---

A chimney that vents outdoors beats a recirculating fan that only traps some grease. Clean the mesh so air can move. Run it for a few minutes after the pan comes off. If you have no chimney, a window exhaust is the next step you may already own.
