id: cooking-vent
title: Ventilate while cooking
source: WHO household air pollution notes, summarized
url: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/household-air-pollution-and-health
tags: cooking kitchen ventilation
---

Frying and frying oil send PM2.5 into the kitchen. Run the exhaust or chimney you already have, and keep the cook in the plume for less time. If outdoor air is also dirty, do not open every window. Close them after the smoke clears. A missing fan is not solved by a houseplant.
