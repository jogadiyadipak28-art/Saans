title: Recirculate the car air
source: In-cabin exposure studies, summarized
url: https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq
tags: commute car
---

Recirculation lowers how much roadway air enters the cabin. It is not a reason to idle. Open the windows only after you leave the jam, and only if outdoor air is actually better.
