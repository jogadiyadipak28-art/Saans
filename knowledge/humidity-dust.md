title: Dust and damp cloths
source: US EPA indoor air guidance, summarized
url: https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq
tags: indoor dust
---

Very dry indoor air lets fine dust stay aloft. A damp mop does more than a humidifier gadget. Do not buy a humidifier to solve smog. Wipe surfaces, then wash the cloth outside the breathing zone.
