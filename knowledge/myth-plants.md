---
id: myth-plants
title: Plants do not replace a filter
source: US EPA indoor air guidance, summarized
url: https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq
tags: myth plants indoor
---

A few potted plants do not lower room PM2.5 in a useful way. They are not a purifier and they are not a reason to skip sealing, mopping, or a fan you already own. Enjoy the plant, and use a real ventilation plan for smoke.
