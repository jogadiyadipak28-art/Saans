---
id: seal-gaps
title: Seal gaps around windows
source: US EPA indoor air guidance, summarized
url: https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq
tags: indoor windows budget
---

Tape or a cloth roll along the frame cuts the air that leaks in when outdoor PM2.5 is high. Focus on the largest visible gaps first. This does not clean air that is already inside, and it should be paired with a plan for cooking fumes. Sealing is a no-cost step and does not require a purifier.
