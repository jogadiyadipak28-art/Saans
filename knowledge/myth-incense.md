id: myth-incense
title: Incense and dhoop add smoke
source: WHO household air pollution notes, summarized
url: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/household-air-pollution-and-health
tags: myth incense indoor
---

Incense, dhoop, and camphor are indoor combustion. They add particles even when the room smells pleasant. On a high-PM day, skip them. A scent is not a sign that the air is clean.
