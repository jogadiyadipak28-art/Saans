title: Infants and dirty air
source: WHO ambient air quality fact sheet, summarized

tags: infant household
---

Infants breathe faster for their size and cannot wear an adult mask. Keep them out of the kitchen plume and away from open waste fires. This is planning advice, not a diagnosis. Severe breathing trouble needs emergency care, not an app.
