id: myth-jaggery
title: Jaggery does not filter air
source: General nutrition myth check; no clinical air claim
url: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/ambient-(outdoor)-air-quality-and-health
tags: myth food
---

Eating jaggery, lemon, or honey does not remove particles from the lungs. Food is not a mask and not a filter. Saans does not give diet therapy. Reduce the minutes spent in dirty air instead.
