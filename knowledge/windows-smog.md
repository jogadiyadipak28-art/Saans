id: windows-smog
title: When to shut windows
source: US EPA indoor air guidance, summarized
url: https://www.epa.gov/indoor-air-quality-iaq
tags: windows outdoor
---

If outdoor PM2.5 is high, an open window imports that air. Shut them, then cook with the fan you have. Open one window later only if the forecast falls. Sealing is stronger than merely closing, and the engine shows both.
