title: Do not burn waste
source: CPCB public guidance themes, summarized

tags: burning community
---

A small leaf or waste fire puts a large pulse of PM2.5 into the lane. The engine counts one avoided fire as a wide range, not a precise gram. Ask the society or school to post a no-burn note. There is no budget required.
