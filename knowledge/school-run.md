source: CPCB National Air Quality Index overview

tags: school children commute

The school gate is often beside idling vehicles. If the morning forecast dips, leave in the cleaner hour. If every morning hour is severe, keep the trip short and skip roadside play. Schools can move assembly indoors without buying a purifier.
